# Prioritize work

## Use this skill

Use this for a requested ranking or selection from an existing candidate set. Reuse IDs, accepted scope, goals and source records. `find-improvements` discovers candidates; `create-tasks` decomposes selected scope; `plan-phases` groups deliverable milestones. A high priority does not make work ready, and a recommendation is not a delivery commitment.

## Establish constraints and capacity

Read the candidates and current commitments. Preserve owners where established and reconcile duplicates or stale status without silently dropping work. Do not search for another backlog or expand into a repository audit unless requested.

Establish the horizon, capacity by constrained role/resource, required deadlines, work underway and interrupt/support obligations. Inspect recorded facts first; ask only for missing choices that could change selection. Separate estimates, assumptions and observed availability. Unknown capacity can support a provisional ranking, not a feasible commitment.

For each item, check its outcome, remaining effort, uncertainty, dependencies, access or human decisions, and required review/acceptance work. Distinguish genuine obligations from preferences. A mandatory date needs a source; a low score cannot make an obligation disappear.

## Compare and select

Compare goal contribution, delay consequence, risk, confidence, remaining cost and enabled work. Use the team's established method when it fits, or a simple explanation. Do not invent inputs to complete a formula or convert incompatible effort bands, points and elapsed waits into guessed hours.

For capacity limits, competing commitments or shared prerequisites, use [the selection playbook](references/selection.playbook.md). Include preparation, integration, review and acceptance. Count a shared prerequisite once, allocate its work to the correct role, and avoid counting the same benefit through duplicate candidates. Team capacity is not interchangeable across specialties.

Show selected work, feasible order and the first ready item or batch. Mark selected-but-blocked work with its unlocking condition. For work underway, consider remaining value and restart costs; sunk effort alone does not justify continuation. Expose a conflict when required work cannot fit instead of silently dropping scope or overloading the plan.

Give deferrals a reason and reconsideration trigger. Preserve unresolved ties controlled by missing evidence or a real decision. Investigate only uncertainties that could change selection; do not decompose every candidate into tasks.

## Result and verification

Return the ranking or selection, rationale, capacity assumptions, ready versus blocked work, deferrals and exact gaps. Follow the requested audience/depth and existing planning format; keep the authorized backlog as the source of record.

Walk the selected set against commitments, dependencies and role capacity. Test a plausible change to the weakest estimate or availability assumption and state which choices would change. A planning assessment cannot prove delivery or guarantee estimates.

Before acceptance, have a separate agent in fresh context challenge the raw candidates, goals, constraints, sources and candidate recommendation without the author's conversation or preferred conclusion. Ask it to test omitted commitments, unsupported scores, resource conflicts and dependency errors. Retain its returned reviewer/session identity, evaluated artifact or revision, findings and coverage. Reconcile defects and independently recheck affected selections after fixes. Without that assessment, report unreviewed; required human decisions remain separate.

Reordering a tracker, assigning people, sending messages or starting work needs the actual authority. For an authorized update, inspect current state and conflict controls, then read back the applied result. A ranking-only request ends with its recommendation.

## Next steps

Pass selected IDs, rationale, constraints and exact prerequisites to `create-tasks` for decomposition, `plan-phases` for milestones, or `implement-change` for an accepted ready task whose execution is authorized. Check availability or describe the plain action. Continue from accepted context; no feasible selection is a valid result when constraints conflict.
