# Handoff

## Goal and scope

Requested outcome, accepted constraints, authorization and stopping point.

## Current state

Repository/workspace, relevant revision or baseline, intended local edits, completed work, partial work, and pending operations with actual IDs.

## Decisions and context

Accepted choices, relevant authoritative artifacts, changed assumptions, and unresolved questions. Include only history that affects continuation.

## Verification

Commands or observed behavior, exit/result, what they establish, and failed/unavailable checks.

## Next action

The next runnable step, its prerequisites and owner where known, remaining dependency order, and the exact blocker if it cannot run.
