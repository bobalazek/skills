# Plan content strategy

## Establish the editorial decision

Produce an evidence-grounded direction and ordered content inventory at the requested scope. Reuse accepted audience, positioning, goals, voice and existing strategy; do not restart settled discovery. Identify the reader need, intended outcome, destination and available production capacity. Inspect supplied content and sources before asking for discoverable facts. A small decision needs no new calendar or document tree.

Ask about consequential unresolved audience, outcome or resource choices and wait before dependent selection; unaffected inventory work or requested options may continue. If choices are delegated, make proportionate recommendations with their assumptions. For one landing page's message and section order, use `plan-landing-page` if available, or plan that page directly. A requested outline alone belongs to its writing action.

## Compare existing content and missing needs

For an existing site or publication, inspect the relevant inventory and record coverage: which pages or pieces were read, their purpose, currency, overlap, owner and available evidence. For a new collection, distinguish proposed content from observed gaps. Use [the inventory decision matrix](references/inventory-decisions.matrix.md) when choosing among keeping, updating, combining, retiring and creating material.

Connect each proposed theme and item to a specific reader question and the accepted outcome. Separate observed requests, content facts and measured results from inferred opportunities. Search queries, support records and interviews establish different kinds of evidence; no one signal proves demand or the cause of poor performance. Zero traffic alone is insufficient reason to remove useful material. Do not invent customer language, keyword volumes, competitor omissions, testimonials or performance gains. Verify current sources when factual research is required; mark unavailable evidence explicitly.

## Select a workable inventory

Compare reader usefulness, expected contribution to the goal, evidence strength, effort and dependencies. Explain priority without treating uncertain impact as a measured score. Reuse useful existing content before adding competing pieces. Give each selected item its reader, angle, proposed treatment, evidence/source needs and next owner; identify unassigned ownership rather than inventing a commitment. Keep blocked high-value work distinct from work ready to commission.

Include relationships between pieces where they help the reader navigate or avoid duplication. Dates, channels, cadence, topic clusters and numeric quotas are conditional choices, not required outputs. Use actual capacity and accepted commitments if a schedule is requested. Do not impose a sales CTA on support or educational content, or turn search suggestions into mandatory keyword placement. Retiring or merging content proposes a transition and affected-link check; it does not authorize deletion or redirects.

## Check and return the strategy

Return the direction, relevant inventory decisions, ordered items, reasons and unresolved evidence in the existing or requested format. Check each priority against the sources and constraints, overlap and maintenance needs, and walk the next selected item into commissioning: can its owner tell what to produce and what remains missing? Report inspected coverage and checks; an expert walkthrough does not prove audience response, rankings or conversion lift.

Before acceptance, obtain a separate fresh-context assessment against the raw request, sources and candidate inventory. Retain the returned assessment and artifact identity, resolve supported defects and recheck affected decisions. Without it, label the strategy unreviewed. This check adds no mandatory human approval beyond the project's actual rules.

Use `write-content-brief` for an accepted item needing a writer assignment; use the relevant writing action when the brief already suffices. Channel/distribution planning, publication and implementation are separate results. Check sibling availability or describe the plain action. Stop at a strategy-only request, or continue ready work already authorized, carrying accepted choices, sources and exact gaps.
