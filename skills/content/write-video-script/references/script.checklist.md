# Video script checks

Use the existing result record and the requested scope. Checked means demonstrated with evidence; failed or unavailable checks stay unchecked with consequence and next action. Explain exclusions rather than checking them off.

## Intent, substance and packaging

- [ ] The original request, audience, format, voice and requested scope survive the draft.
- [ ] The actual requested artifact is present: full wording and visual beats through the ending for a script, scoped options for a hook request, or the requested outline; advice/placeholders do not substitute for it.
- [ ] Requested destination/surface variants contain the needed adapted words and complete endings while preserving the accepted purpose.
- [ ] Material questions and story feedback are recorded; the concept/message/payoff is confirmed or explicitly delegated before dependent production, otherwise clearly proposed.
- [ ] Claims have sources or clear fiction/hypothesis labels; missing demonstrations remain gaps.
- [ ] Title/cover or opening-frame expectations match what the piece can deliver.

## Hook and continuation

- [ ] The opening makes the subject and relevance intelligible without unnecessary delay or empty curiosity.
- [ ] Spoken words, first visual and optional screen text support the same honest promise.
- [ ] The next beat gives useful progress rather than another unsupported teaser.

## Body and payoff

- [ ] Each substantive beat adds explanation, evidence, action or consequence.
- [ ] Learning prerequisites, causal order and material qualifications are preserved.
- [ ] The ending fulfills the precise promise; any CTA or loop leaves a complete experience on one viewing.

## Performance and handoff

- [ ] Exact words, delivery/phonetic directions, visual actions and sound events are separate.
- [ ] Timing is labeled with the method actually used and allows for action, pauses and reading.
- [ ] Needed shots and audible events have feasible sources or explicit production gaps; silent/interview formats are respected.
- [ ] Required review has a returned assessment tied to the script revision, or the draft remains unreviewed; no playback or audience-impact claim exceeds the evidence.
- [ ] The handoff identifies accepted words/IDs, remaining choices and dependent timing/caption checks without starting unrequested production.
