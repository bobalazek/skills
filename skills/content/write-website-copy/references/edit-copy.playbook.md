# Edit website copy in context

Use for substantive page drafting, revision or a website-specific critique. Reuse the accepted page purpose, facts and content plan. General prose review belongs to `review-writing` when available; this procedure checks what website wording commits the product to.

## Check the page's promise

Read the passage with its heading, arrival promise, surrounding sections and action. Does it explain the supported offer to this audience? Preserve useful navigation labels and consistent product terms. Resolve missing section strategy through page planning rather than disguising it with a stronger headline.

Trace factual claims to approved evidence. A capability demonstration cannot establish an unmeasured customer outcome. Retain material costs, eligibility and limitations near the claim. Keep unsupported proof out of the draft and name the missing fact separately.

For an opening, check that the continuation delivers its promise. Compare every call to action with its actual destination and consequence. Do not change an offer through wording, such as turning a sales inquiry into an immediate free trial. Where context is unavailable, mark that exact check as incomplete.

## Synthetic example

Assume a fictional calendar tool is verified to flag overlapping events before import and let the user choose what to import. The destination is a recorded demo; attendance outcomes are unmeasured.

"Never miss a meeting again. Start your free trial" becomes "See overlapping events before you import them. Choose what enters your calendar. Watch the import demo."

The edit replaces an unsupported outcome with demonstrated behavior and corrects the action. An accurate, distinctive line such as "Import day should not start with detective work" can remain when it fits the requested voice.

## Return and verify

Return the requested words at their existing content keys, or exact quoted findings for a critique-only request. Keep support notes separate from public copy. Compare against the original facts, material conditions and meaning. Reuse a valid independent writing review; the owning skill still checks offer accuracy, destination behavior, requested rendering and completion. A prose review cannot prove fit or conversion results.
