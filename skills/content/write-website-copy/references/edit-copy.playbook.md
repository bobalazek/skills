# Draft and edit website copy

Use for substantive website drafting or revision, or a requested wording critique. Reuse the accepted page purpose, facts and content plan. This procedure finishes words for the requested locations; unresolved section strategy belongs to page planning.

## Choose the editing job

For new copy, start from the supported answer the reader needs. Name the subject, useful mechanism or consequence, then connect it to the actual action. A supplied reference can inform tone or structure; it is not permission to reuse distinctive wording or evidence.

For a human draft, first identify the voice to keep: characteristic vocabulary, rhythm, humor, directness and meaningful uncertainty. Preserve strong accurate passages. Change a sentence because it obscures meaning, repeats information, misstates a fact or conflicts with the requested voice, rather than making every sentence equally polished. Preserve the requested format and existing content keys.

For critique only, quote the exact passage, explain its concrete problem and give a local repair or the missing fact. Report no supported findings when the wording holds. Do not replace the whole draft without that request. Writing patterns do not establish AI authorship; do not provide an authorship verdict or score.

## Make each passage useful

Read the section in context, including its heading, preceding promise and action. Apply only the checks relevant to the actual problem:

| Symptom | Check and repair |
| --- | --- |
| Interchangeable praise | If another product could use the sentence unchanged, ask what useful information it carries. Replace it with a supported capability or consequence, or remove it. A necessary plain label can still be generic. |
| An opening that delays the subject | Put the useful point where the reader needs it. Keep a story or question when it helps this audience understand the offer; remove empty announcements of importance. |
| Vague evidence or authority | Trace the precise claim to its source and scope. Narrow it or mark the gap; "research shows" cannot replace the research. |
| Abstract benefit or unexplained jargon | Show what the reader can actually do, using their vocabulary. Retain a necessary specialist term with an explanation rather than replacing it with an inaccurate synonym. |
| Repeated meaning or uniform sentence shapes | Cut duplication and read the passage aloud. Keep intentional rhythm; do not impose a word count, punctuation ban or uniform sentence length. |
| Inflated contrast or an unearned ending | State the supported difference or useful next step. Keep a real comparison when it aids a decision; remove a dramatic reversal that the facts do not support. |
| An action that promises the wrong result | Compare the label with the actual destination and consequence. Explain relevant commitment nearby rather than adding unsupported reassurance. |

These are editing signals, not a universal blacklist. Keep a phrase when its meaning, audience and project voice justify it. Specificity must come from evidence; do not invent a metric, example, opinion or customer story to make a sentence sound less generic.

## Check the whole edit

Compare the changed copy with its source facts and original meaning. Confirm that qualifications, uncertainty and useful voice survived; a shorter sentence can still be less accurate. Read the heading and action together, then the passage in order: does it answer the section's question, and does the continuation deliver the opening's promise?

Return the actual requested text, preserving the requested format. Explain only material changes and exact gaps. For a critique, use the quoted findings instead. Keep internal evidence notes out of publishable copy. Rendered fit, destination checks and the independent assessment remain part of the owning skill's completion requirements.

## Synthetic example

Assume a fictional calendar tool is verified to flag overlapping events before import and let the user choose which events to import. Its later outcomes have not been measured.

**Draft:** "Unlock effortless scheduling with our revolutionary platform. Never miss a meeting again. Get started."

**Edited:** "See overlapping events before you import them. Choose which events to bring into your calendar." If the linked destination is a recorded demonstration, use "Watch the import demo" for the action.

The edit names demonstrated behavior, removes the unsupported attendance promise and matches the action to its destination. If the original also contains a distinctive accurate line such as "Import day should not start with detective work," retain it when it fits the requested voice. A copy cleanup does not require replacing every human sentence.
