# Page content plan

Use for a complete page or a handoff. Reuse the project's brief and omit fields already covered by authoritative links.

## Context

- Page and revision; new page or proposed change to an existing one.
- Visitor task, audience, arrival source/promise and current alternative; evidence or assumption for each consequential choice.
- Verified offer, limits, central message and public proof sources.
- Main action and destination; confirmed commitment, cost and what happens next. Other valid visitor paths to preserve.
- Existing voice, terminology, locale and content constraints.

## Content sequence

| Order / section | Visitor question | Required answer or claim | Evidence and public-use status | Useful next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing or proposed section ID | What the visitor needs to understand | Meaning to convey; final prose can follow | Accepted source, observable demonstration or exact gap | Real destination/action, or none |

For example, a synthetic calendar product with a demonstrated conflict preview can answer “Will this replace my calendar?” by explaining the confirmed preview workflow. It cannot claim fewer missed meetings without outcome evidence. A short demonstration may be enough; an invented testimonial is not a substitute.

## Handoff

Record meaningful changes and omissions with reasons, unresolved facts/choices and their owners, and the paths checked. Link the independent assessment to the plan revision. Name the next missing output without repeating its procedure. Keep plan approval, copy readiness, rendered verification and measured outcomes distinct.

## Guidance behind the checks

- [GOV.UK user needs](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs) grounds content in actual tasks and constraints. Internal assumptions need evidence.
- [Google Ads landing-page guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6238826?hl=en) supports matching the arrival promise and making needed information and actions easy to find. It does not establish a universal section sequence or conversion improvement.
- [W3C link purpose](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/link-purpose-in-context.html) supports understandable destinations. A content plan alone cannot establish rendered accessibility.
