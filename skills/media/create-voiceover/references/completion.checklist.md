# Narration completion

Apply at the requested fidelity: audition, scratch or final. Reuse the manifest/result record. Check only demonstrated criteria with evidence; failures and unavailable checks stay unchecked with consequence and next action. Explain exclusions.

## Script and source

- [ ] Accepted words, language, purpose, audience and required output are identified; wording changes return to the script owner.
- [ ] Stable script IDs survive the chosen line/paragraph/continuous recording units; useful supplied takes are reused.
- [ ] Clean dialogue, pronunciation aliases and delivery directions are separate; controls do not leak into spoken words or captions.
- [ ] Source/voice choice, consent, rights, current provider capabilities, disclosure and spending authority cover this use.
- [ ] Submitted job identity and output are reconciled before retrying an ambiguous paid request.

## Files and timing

- [ ] Selected files decode and contain the intended audio; originals and selected take identities are retained.
- [ ] Measured source ranges, unit durations and stem positions reconcile, with estimates and scratch material clearly labeled.
- [ ] Actual words are checked against accepted text; meaning-changing mismatches are resolved and caption guidance follows selected speech.
- [ ] Loudness and true peak were measured against a verified destination requirement or explicit assumption, including any requested mix.
- [ ] Changed units identify affected downstream timing, captions, sync and exports; anchored material is not assumed to shift sequentially.

## Listening and delivery

- [ ] Actual listening covers all selected speech, names/numbers, boundaries, tone, noise and intended pauses; ASR is only corroboration.
- [ ] Requested music has appropriate rights and does not mask speech; voice/music/mix files are distinct when needed.
- [ ] Listening coverage identifies who heard what and where; unheard material remains unchecked even when technical checks pass.
- [ ] Required independent assessment has returned findings, reviewer identity, revision and coverage, or the result remains unreviewed.
- [ ] Delivered files and manifest agree on scratch/final status, selected version and exact gaps; production or publication beyond this job is not implied.
