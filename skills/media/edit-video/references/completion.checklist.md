# Video edit completion

Use the existing edit record and requested scope; a caption repair need not reopen the full story. Checked means demonstrated with evidence for the current files. Failures and unavailable checks stay unchecked with consequence and next action. Explain exclusions.

## Sources and intent

- [ ] Video type, purpose, audience and requested edit remain intact; accepted material outside a repair stays unchanged.
- [ ] Originals and source identities are preserved; actual tracks, timing basis, orientation and duration were inspected.
- [ ] Selected passages retain meaning, context and qualifiers, with unambiguous source occurrences.
- [ ] Every required action, demonstration and payoff has inspected coverage or a named gap.
- [ ] The opening/packaging promise, first useful continuation and complete ending agree; tightening or teasing has not removed prerequisites, proof conditions or the payoff.

## Timeline and sound

- [ ] Source ranges and output positions reconcile after cuts, speed changes and reordering; separate sound has observed offsets and drift checks.
- [ ] Native dialogue/sound, narration, music and silence have explicit track decisions, without missing or duplicate speech.
- [ ] Joins preserve words, intentional pauses and movement; processing does not damage intelligibility.
- [ ] Crops preserve moving subjects and essential information; required redactions cover entire affected intervals.
- [ ] Captions reflect actual speech on the final timeline, retain proper spellings and exclude performance tags; changed media invalidates affected cues.

## Export and handoff

- [ ] File/stream presence, decodability, expected duration and dimensions pass technical checks for each requested variant.
- [ ] Actual playback covers joins, repairs, opening, ending and destination-size text/framing; missing observations remain explicit.
- [ ] Listening covers clipped words, clicks, sync, mix and unintended silence in the actual export; measurements alone do not close it.
- [ ] Caption tracks and requested accessibility companions were checked with their exact companion files.
- [ ] Independent assessment has a returned result with reviewer, revision, findings and actual playback/listening coverage, or remains open.
- [ ] Delivered files and editable decisions match the checked revision, with rights, tool/interchange limits and remaining gaps; publication was not inferred from edit authority.
