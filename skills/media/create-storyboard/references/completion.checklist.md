# Storyboard completion

Use the existing record at the requested fidelity. Checked means demonstrated with evidence. Failed or unavailable checks stay unchecked with consequence and next action; explain exclusions.

## Intent and coverage

- [ ] Opening words/picture/text establish one supported promise; the first useful continuation and payoff map to actual shots or named gaps without repeated empty teasers.
- [ ] Video type, goal, audience, destination and accepted script/brief are preserved; material missing choices were raised.
- [ ] Each shot has a narrative job and identifiable opening, action and ending states.
- [ ] All important actions, proof and payoff beats have coverage or explicit production gaps; reframing does not conceal missing events.
- [ ] Planned visuals, inspected sources and produced assets have distinct statuses.
- [ ] Human feedback and material visual choices are recorded as proposed, accepted, delegated or open; dependent production waits for unresolved required choices.

## Composition and dependencies

- [ ] Each requested layout preserves essential action and readable text; visual inspection is distinguished from a written intention.
- [ ] Relevant identity, props, location, screen direction and state connect across shots and transitions.
- [ ] References name actual available files and roles, with rights/privacy limits and missing inputs identified.
- [ ] Native speech/sound, external narration, music and silence have clear dependencies, including visible-dialogue sync.
- [ ] Timings state their actual basis; estimates allow for action, pauses and reading and await measured media where necessary.

## Assessment and handoff

- [ ] Every requested panel and meaningful join was reviewed at the available fidelity; absent visual/playback proof remains open.
- [ ] Required independent assessment identifies the reviewed version, reviewer, findings and coverage, or the board remains unreviewed.
- [ ] Production has accepted inputs, stable shot IDs, asset gaps and a feasible next action; no finished-media or provider-execution claim exceeds the evidence.
