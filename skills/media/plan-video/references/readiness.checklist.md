# Video planning checks

Apply to the requested scope in the existing result record. Check only demonstrated items with evidence; failed or unavailable items stay unchecked with the consequence and next action. Mark exclusions with a reason.

## Audience and ideas

- [ ] The original request, intended viewer/reward and stopping point remain intact.
- [ ] Proposed concepts differ in premise or reward; variants are identified as variants.
- [ ] The recommendation explains its fit and main constraint without inventing acceptance.
- [ ] Topic, depth and payoff serve the accepted primary goal; any proposed performance measure distinguishes that outcome from attention or engagement proxies.
- [ ] Material discovery questions were answered, explicitly delegated or left open; the human's concept/promise choice is recorded before dependent work.
- [ ] Open questions identify the script, board or production step that must wait; asking has not been treated as receiving an answer.
- [ ] The brief preserves useful options, rationale, accepted changes and the next unresolved checkpoint without duplicating private chat.

## Substance and format

- [ ] Sources support the claims, with transcript/visual/analytics access distinguished.
- [ ] Format, production method, visual style and layout are separate choices that serve the piece.
- [ ] Purpose and destination/surface are explicit; requested channel adaptations preserve a complete promise/payoff rather than assuming an identical crop or truncated master works everywhere.
- [ ] The angle offers useful substance and the packaging promise has a feasible payoff.
- [ ] Fiction, hypotheses, planned demonstrations and observed results are distinguished.

## Readiness

- [ ] Required actions and proof have usable assets or named acquisition gaps; rights and privacy are considered.
- [ ] Length, language, sound mode, destination and production capacity are compatible or unresolved choices are explicit.
- [ ] A series/batch fits the available production and review capacity; selecting it implies no new spending authority.
- [ ] The handoff carries accepted inputs, checks and the next useful action; required independent assessment has an actual returned result or remains open.
