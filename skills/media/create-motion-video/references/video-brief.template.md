# Video brief

Use the existing production brief when it fits. Ask for missing consequential choices; omit irrelevant fields. Save in the consuming project, not the installed skill.

## Purpose and scope

- Requested result: storyboard, preview, final export or scoped revision
- Subject, supported message and intended viewer action/understanding:
- Audience, language, destination and viewing conditions:
- Existing composition/script and behavior to preserve:
- Accepted choices, unresolved questions and responsible decision maker:

## Output contract

| Format | Width × height | FPS | Frame count | Duration = count / FPS | Container/codecs | Captions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Requested destination | Integer pixels | Fixed rate | Agreed rounding | Seconds | Supported target | Burned-in, sidecar or none |

- Audio: silent, supplied narration, authorized generated narration or music-led
- Audio source/version, measured duration and cue confidence:
- Output/source locations, available renderer/browser/encoder and versions:
- Render time/storage budget, preview scope and stopping condition:

## Content and look

| Claim or asset | Evidence/source | Approved use and attribution | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product claim, recording, logo, font, screenshot or music | Actual source | License/permission and scope | Unsupported or unavailable |

- Applicable voice guide and script status:
- Style references inspected, captured coverage and desired adaptations:
- Palette, geometry, typography, texture and motion behavior:
- Optional direction: cut paper, crosshatch, risograph, sketchbook, isometric or supplied look; these are choices, not bundled renderers
- Character/hero anchor: proportions, identity, recurring details and permitted variations
- Screen text, readable holds, contrast, flashes and caption requirements:
