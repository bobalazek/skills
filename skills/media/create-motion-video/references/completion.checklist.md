# Motion video completion

Apply only to the requested artifact: a storyboard needs preview checks, not a claim of final playback. Use the existing production record. Check demonstrated items with evidence tied to the current source/export; failed or unavailable checks stay unchecked with consequence and next action. Explain exclusions.

## Brief and story

- [ ] Video type, goal, audience, destination, duration and audio mode are settled or material gaps are explicit.
- [ ] Opening, progression and payoff deliver the original request with supported claims and preserved qualifications.
- [ ] Each important action/proof beat has actual asset coverage; planned or missing material is labeled.
- [ ] Asset identity, reference role, rights and forbidden/private content have been checked.

## Timing and composition

- [ ] Narration uses measured selected audio; scratch and estimated cues remain labeled.
- [ ] Beat timing covers meaningful action, reading and pauses; script/audio changes invalidate affected cues and captions.
- [ ] Every material beat and transition has a checked preview; actual text fits each requested layout.
- [ ] Product screens remain authentic and comprehensible; recaptures recheck pixel-dependent motion.

## Export and assessment

- [ ] Deterministic checks confirm files, decode, duration, dimensions, required streams and missing-asset failures before subjective acceptance.
- [ ] Each requested export was inspected at normal speed and destination size; stills alone are not playback proof.
- [ ] Applicable listening checks cover actual words, pronunciation, mix and sync; unheard audio remains unchecked.
- [ ] Captions and requested accessibility companions match the final media and do not obstruct essential content.
- [ ] Returned independent assessment identifies the source/export revision, reviewer, findings and coverage; required rechecks are complete or open.
- [ ] Editable source, reproducible render inputs/command and requested outputs are usable; delivery status and publication authority are distinct.
