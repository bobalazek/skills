# Cost and obligation checks

Load the relevant parts when they can change a project decision.

## Cost

State currency, units, period, price source/date, workload assumptions, and scenario. Separate one-time build/migration effort from recurring infrastructure, vendors, support, and operating work. Include meaningful limits, scaling steps, minimum charges, retention/egress, and switching cost where applicable. Show arithmetic and uncertainty; do not invent usage or precision. A free tier is not a production capacity assumption.

## Obligations

Establish users/jurisdictions, data categories, distribution, business/customer contracts, dependencies/licenses, and regulated activities before making applicability claims. Consult current primary texts and official guidance. Record obligation, applicability evidence, implementation implication, decision owner, and unresolved interpretation. Avoid declaring compliance from a generic checklist or treating a software assessment as legal approval.

## Security and operation

Identify assets, trust boundaries, access/tenancy, data lifecycle, abuse paths, and the controls the selected design needs. Check how controls will be verified and operated. Unresolved acceptance of a material security or legal risk belongs to its authorized owner.
