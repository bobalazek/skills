# Site inspection

Use the accepted audience, offer and primary action to choose the depth. Keep route/state observations in the existing audit record; a lens is a question to investigate, not an automatic defect.

## Establish a representative sample

Start with the visitor's likely entry and follow one primary journey to its authorized endpoint. Include a representative offer/detail page, decision/checkout/contact page, and a shared navigation or footer surface. Add other page types, locales, device/input conditions or signed-in states when they materially change the journey. Record inaccessible routes and unsampled variants.

Capture the actual URL and state before interpreting a result. For each step, note what information is presented, what action is offered, where it leads, and what success or failure becomes visible. A button labeled Book may open an inquiry rather than complete a booking; judge that difference against the promised result and business contract.

## Inspect nine lenses

| Lens | Procedure and useful evidence | Interpretation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First impression | Read the initial view and headings; identify the offered service/product, intended audience, important qualification and next action. Compare with the supplied brief and actual destination. | A quick expert impression is not a timed participant study or proof that users understand the offer. |
| Design quality | Inspect hierarchy, type readability, spacing, images and cohesion across sampled pages. Check relevant narrow/wide layouts, themes and content extremes for overflow or obscured controls. | Select supported widths and states from the product; do not impose a favorite aesthetic or fixed viewport pair. |
| Navigation and usability | Traverse the primary path, back/exit routes, forms and relevant validation/recovery. Count actual steps where friction matters; inspect labels, feedback and required information at the decision point. | More clicks or fields are not inherently defects: necessary qualification, consent or recovery can justify them. Stop before unauthorized submissions. |
| Content | Compare headings, offer details, pricing/eligibility, calls to action and proof with accepted business facts. Quote confusing, contradictory, stale or unsupported claims and locate them. | Writing patterns cannot establish AI authorship; numbers, endorsements, guarantees and urgency need supporting facts. A copyright year alone does not establish stale content. |
| Search basics | Inspect representative response/indexing directives, titles, headings, descriptive links, meaningful image alternatives, canonical destinations and relevant structured data. Check sitemap/robots coverage when available. | Apply current [Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials). A valid file, heading count or schema snippet does not establish crawl/index/ranking outcomes; retain unavailable search evidence. |
| Performance | Observe loading, visual movement and interaction under stated conditions. When authorized tools exist, measure representative routes and record tool/version, device/network, runs and metric scope. Investigate large media, blocking work or repeated requests using actual evidence. | Distinguish lab results from field measurements and causal hypotheses. [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/defining-core-web-vitals-thresholds) describe specific field metrics; one lab score is neither whole-site performance nor conversion evidence. |
| Accessibility | Exercise relevant keyboard/focus, names/labels, form errors, zoom/reflow, contrast and motion behavior with suitable tools. Follow the applicable standard and criterion rather than substituting visual preference. | Inspect [W3C accessibility evaluation guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/). Limited sampling or automated scans cannot prove conformance. Target-size rules have criterion-specific exceptions; do not declare every control below 44 pixels a failure. |
| Conversion journey | Compare the entry promise with the destination, required steps, disclosures, trust evidence and actual result. Inspect consent/cancellation, error feedback and recovery as well as success within authority. | A visible call to action or successful page load is not a persisted conversion. Essential navigation, legal links and informed refusal are not automatic leaks to remove. |
| Business and local fit | Translate supplied business/audience facts into relevant checks: eligibility, service area, availability, fulfillment, contact, pricing basis or evidence of required credentials. Verify consequential external claims with current primary sources when access is in scope. | Keep wages, customer prices, fees and job values distinct. Record benchmark geography/date/unit and provenance; do not invent local regulations, margins, demand or payback. |

## Reconcile the evidence

Confirm each suspected defect on the relevant route/state, or keep its unverified part explicit. Attribute supplied captures, analytics and owner statements. Match performance comparisons by conditions and distinguish runtime failures from their proposed cause. Preserve actionable minority findings even if other lenses look good.

Treat benefit estimates as hypotheses requiring a baseline and measurement plan. Rank the actual user consequence, reach, available recovery and dependencies; a polished homepage can coexist with a broken primary action. Finishing the audit means returning an honest assessment and useful next work, including missing observations.
