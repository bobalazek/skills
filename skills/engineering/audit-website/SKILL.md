# Audit website

## Establish the audit

Use this for an integrated assessment of a website against its audience, offer and primary goal. Reuse known issues and valid observations. Identify the URL or supplied candidate, business type, audience, intended action, important routes, supported surfaces and available access. Ask about an unresolved goal or missing target before judging its effectiveness; inspect ready evidence while that answer is pending.

Perform the assessment independently of the site's author, with the raw brief and candidate rather than an author's preferred verdict. If independence is unavailable, label the assessment unreviewed. An audit can finish with findings and explicit coverage gaps; it cannot establish release readiness through missing proof.

Agree a representative route and state sample: landing page, a relevant offer/detail page, the decision point and the destination of the primary action, plus consequential error/recovery states. A single homepage cannot establish whole-site coverage. Use synthetic or approved data. Opening a public page does not authorize submitting real leads, booking, purchasing, sending messages, changing data or running intrusive scans. Stop at the consequential boundary unless its test is authorized.

## Inspect the journey and supporting pages

For a multi-page or cross-discipline audit, use the [site inspection playbook](references/site-inspection.playbook.md) to sample routes and work through nine applicable lenses: first impression, design, navigation, content, search basics, performance, accessibility, conversion and business/local fit. For a focused request, select the relevant lenses and explain the remaining coverage.

Walk the primary action through its actual destination; inspect what the visitor knows, must supply and receives at each step. Correlate visible results with relevant response, console or persistence evidence where available. State which observations you made and which were supplied. Static HTML and screenshots support only the properties they expose; they do not establish interactive success, search indexing, accessibility conformance or conversion lift.

Use available authorized browser, HTTP and diagnostic tools appropriate to the claim. Read the project's tools and constraints first; missing tools remain gaps rather than permission to install a new stack. Record route, state, device/viewport, conditions and candidate identity where available. Keep destructive or external effects outside an ordinary audit.

## Connect findings to the goal

Record a located observation, its affected task and consequence, evidence, useful correction and priority. Use project priority labels; otherwise P0 critical, P1 high, P2 normal and P3 low, with an impact/urgency rationale. Keep priority separate from severity, confidence and readiness. Preserve finding IDs through rechecks and explain any changed rating.

Combine symptoms of one cause across lenses without losing route/state coverage. Separate broken journeys, accessibility barriers and misleading claims from aesthetic preferences or untested hypotheses. Do not infer user emotions, actual lost revenue or AI authorship from appearance or prose. Business benchmarks need a source, date, geography, unit and limits. Unknown conversion, capacity, close-rate, price or margin inputs remain unknown; modeled scenarios are not measured outcomes.

## Return a useful audit

Use the existing report format, or the [audit report template](references/website-audit-report.template.md) when a structured report is needed. Include the scoped conclusion, prioritized findings, inspected and uninspected coverage, and an action plan linked to finding IDs. Estimate effort only from the actual change and known constraints; state uncertainty and dependencies. Each material finding needs evidence, and each unavailable required observation needs its consequence and next useful check. Omit generic recommendations unsupported by this site.

Before handing off, reconcile the selected routes and lenses with the evidence. Preserve failed attempts and distinguish observed defects from suspected causes. Do not claim independent review, live testing or a performance measurement without its actual record.

For selected repairs, use `implement-change`; for a focused rendered-experience review, use `review-interface`; for unexplained runtime failure, use `diagnose-issue`. A requested security audit belongs to `audit-security`; deeper search research or testing needs a separately scoped search assessment. Check sibling availability or describe the plain next action. An audit-only request ends with its findings and recommendation; continue only work already authorized.
