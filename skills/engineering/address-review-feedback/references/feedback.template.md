# Review feedback resolution

Reuse the existing PR, issue or task record. Identify the supplied review, reviewed and current revisions, accepted requirements and permitted actions.

| Finding / thread | Current evidence and disposition | Correction or reason | Verification / independent recheck | Reply / remote state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Original identity | Supported, already addressed, unsupported, deferred or blocked | Smallest fix, source-backed disagreement or missing decision | Actual result and revision, or exact gap | Draft, sent or resolved only when observed and authorized |

Link duplicate findings to one shared cause. Record who accepted a deferral and why; unresolved risk is not an accepted deferral. Preserve failed checks and refresh affected proof after edits.

Finish with unresolved decisions, unavailable checks and the next authorized action. Distinguish implemented, verified, independently reviewed and remotely resolved states.
