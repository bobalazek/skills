# Identity handoff

Use for reusable identity guidelines or asset delivery. Reuse the authoritative project format and include only the touchpoints and deliverables requested.

## Brief and decision status

Audience, accepted positioning, intended associations, retained assets, requested change and actual use contexts. Identify accepted, proposed and delegated choices and consequential unresolved direction.

## Visual rules

| Element | Rule and rationale | Source or master | Applicable use and exceptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mark, palette, typography, imagery or icons | A concrete choice tied to the brief | Located source/asset | Actual sizes, backgrounds, channels or scripts |

Include a recurring character's visual invariants only when in scope. Keep functional accessibility roles separate from decorative identity choices; list relevant restrictions and fallbacks.

## Asset manifest

| Asset/variant | Editable source | Export and intended use | Rights/attribution | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Requested item | Actual master path and format | Actual output path/format, background and size | Located licence or exact unresolved gap | Exploration, reviewed, owner-accepted or unchecked |

Do not list uncreated files as delivered. Distinguish a raster concept, editable master and production export. A successful export does not establish trademark clearance or rights to third-party material.

## Usage evidence

Record the inspected candidate revision, representative applications and actual source/export checks. Include size/background samples, clipping/readability, transparency, font fallback and script coverage where relevant. Record applicable contrast checks without implying a comprehensive accessibility audit. Link the returned independent assessment and unresolved findings when required; do not invent acceptance or audience response.

## Ownership and handoff

Authoritative asset/guideline location, owner, update process, accepted decisions and exact next work. For a requested rollout, list affected touchpoints, old/new assets and replacement checks. Keep production replacement or purchases outside the draft's authority unless already authorized.
