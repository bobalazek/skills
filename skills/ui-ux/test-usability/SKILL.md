# Test usability

## Use this skill

Use this for a requested protocol or findings about how people attempt a defined task. Start from accepted requirements and available sessions; analysis does not automatically require new recruitment. `review-interface` owns expert assessment, while `map-user-flows` and `design-interface` own proposed product changes.

A plan, simulated persona or agent walkthrough cannot establish user success. A requested plan can finish as a plan; a requested study remains incomplete without required participant observations.

## Frame the decision and method

Identify the decision, relevant roles/context, target revision and consequential tasks. Reuse previous findings and inspect actual behavior where available. Preserve relevant workflows and distinguish a prototype limitation from a product failure.

Choose a method that answers the question: task observation for completion/recovery, a tree test for findability, or comprehension tasks for labels/consequences. Interviews explain expectations but do not prove completion. Match participants and depth to consequential audience differences; do not impose a universal count or promise statistical confidence from a small qualitative study.

For task preparation or synthesis across sessions, use [the study playbook](references/study.playbook.md). Keep the research focused on the requested decision rather than expanding into market research.

## Prepare neutral tasks and authorized sessions

Check participant access, available tools, safe environment and permission before activity. Recruitment, recording, sharing and live-account actions need their actual authority. Follow agreed consent, storage, access and retention arrangements; explain the activity and honor withdrawal. Use synthetic or approved data and avoid real charges, messages, credential disclosure or irreversible actions.

Write realistic goals without naming the control or route under test. Define observable success, including any required terminal result, and failure before the session. Rehearse instructions and environment, labeling rehearsal separately from participant evidence.

During authorized sessions, record attempts, outcomes, assistance, recovery and uncertainty. Do not coach success or silently change tasks between people. Preserve material deviations. If participants, consent, access or recordings are unavailable, produce useful protocol work or analyze only supplied evidence, naming what cannot be established.

## Synthesize what was observed

Link findings to tasks, anonymized sessions, actual actions and capture conditions. Separate participant statements, observed behavior, interpretation and proposed remedies. Report unaided completion, assisted completion, unsuccessful attempts and missing observations distinctly; a participant's impression cannot substitute for an unobserved terminal result.

Retain conflicting observations and isolated severe barriers. Use counts and denominators only when records support them. State coverage limits, repeat exposure and changed conditions where relevant. Do not generalize convenience participants to the audience, claim improvement without a comparable baseline, or treat usability findings as comprehensive accessibility conformance.

## Result and verification

Return the protocol or supported findings, decision informed, method/coverage, evidence and exact gaps at the requested depth and project format. Update an authorized research record instead of duplicating summaries. Recommendations must follow observations and identify the next evidence needed for consequential uncertainty.

Before acceptance, have a separate agent in fresh context challenge the raw request, task definitions, candidate and permitted source evidence without the author's conversation or preferred answer. Respect consent/access limits when sharing. It must check neutrality, traceability, completion claims and contradictions. Retain the returned reviewer/session identity, evaluated artifact/revision, findings and coverage. Reconcile defects and independently recheck affected results after fixes. Missing review means unreviewed; missing required observations means incomplete. Required human approval remains separate.

For authorized PR work, include relevant redacted observations and actual independent findings with accessible evidence, refreshed after material changes. Do not publish participant records just to make a PR self-contained.

## Next steps

Pass supported findings, affected tasks/revision and evidence limits to `map-user-flows` for changed navigation/recovery, `design-interface` for an agreed screen revision, or `write-spec` for unsettled required behavior. Use `review-interface` for a separate expert assessment. Name the unresolved choice or prerequisite, check availability or give the plain action. Finish at the requested research result, or continue ready work already authorized.
