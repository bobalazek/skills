# Review report

## Scope and verdict

Reviewed revision/baseline, intent, surfaces, and whether evidence supports the requested readiness claim. State blocking gaps rather than an unsupported approval.

## Findings

For each: priority, concise problem, affected scenario, file/symbol location, evidence, consequence, and correction or next check. Deduplicate root causes. Keep optional adjacent improvements separate.

## Verification and coverage

Map material criteria to observed commands/results or runtime evidence and identify the tested revision/environment. Include useful screenshot or before/after measurement links, comparison conditions, reviewed surfaces, and uninspected areas. Separate failed or unavailable required checks from optional coverage limits. Identify stale evidence or local-only artifacts; no findings in the inspected scope does not establish whole-system correctness.

## Next action

Selected fixes or investigations, accepted residual risks and their owner where established, and readiness for the specifically requested next step.
