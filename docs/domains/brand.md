# Brand

This domain owns strategic positioning and visual identity. Its packages can run independently from the consuming project's facts and accepted decisions; they do not require Corco records or a full collection install.

## Categories and skills

| Category | Subcategory | Skill | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy | Define positioning | [define-brand-positioning](../../skills/brand/define-brand-positioning/SKILL.md) | An evidence-grounded audience, alternatives, meaningful difference, promise and proof brief, with proposed/accepted status and exact uncertainty |
| Visual identity | Design and govern assets | [design-brand-identity](../../skills/brand/design-brand-identity/SKILL.md) | The requested visual direction, reusable guidelines or editable identity assets, with inspected uses, source/export status and rights gaps |

## Boundaries and use

`define-brand-positioning` settles what the business should be known for from the actual offer and audience evidence. It can return provisional options when support is sparse; a coherent statement does not prove demand or product-market fit. [Product](product.md) owns evidence for a product investment and competitor research when those are the requested outputs.

`design-brand-identity` translates accepted meaning and retained assets into a visual language and the requested asset set. It preserves an existing logo or identity when the request calls for rules or a small refresh. A direction draft, exported asset and owner-accepted identity are different completion states.

[Content](content.md) owns writing voice, page messages and written content. [UI/UX](ui-ux.md) owns screen composition and shared component/token contracts. Brand identity can inform those outputs without owning a website redesign or design-system migration.

## Resources and verification

Each package carries a conditional handoff template. Verify strategy claims against supplied evidence; inspect identity assets in the actual uses requested. Keep owner decisions, agent review and audience observations distinct. Do not infer rights, trademark clearance, market demand or conversion gains from attractive wording or graphics.
