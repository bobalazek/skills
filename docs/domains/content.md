# Content

This domain owns audience-facing messages and written content, plus a reusable prose-review workflow for other domains. It covers website planning and copy, blog posts and articles, and writing-quality reviews. Dedicated social-post and video-script workflows are not yet provided.

## Categories and skills

| Category | Subcategory | Skill | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Website content | Plan a page | [plan-landing-page](../../skills/content/plan-landing-page/SKILL.md) | Audience, arrival promise, supported message, section sequence, proof needs and next action |

## Boundaries and use

`plan-landing-page` settles what a page needs to say and prove. `write-website-copy` produces the requested words, including a small rewrite that needs no new plan. Reuse accepted product facts and content; a content draft cannot establish demand, user comprehension or conversion lift.

`write-blog-post` develops an article from supported material. `review-writing` evaluates an existing passage across formats and edits it when requested. Its checks cover generic AI-style wording without claiming to detect authorship. It preserves a writer's useful voice, uncertainty and precise terminology; it does not impose a universal word or punctuation blacklist.

[UI/UX](ui-ux.md) owns journeys, navigation, screen layout and behavior-dependent strings through `write-interface-copy`. A headline explaining an offer belongs to Content; a permission error or submission confirmation belongs to UI/UX, even when it appears on the same page. Reconcile the page's promise with those actual actions and states.

[Product](product.md) owns validation, feedback, competitor evidence and measurement. [Productivity](productivity.md) owns work coordination and operational communication such as status reports and handoffs. Those remain distinct outputs and need no move into Content.

Opening hooks stay within the relevant writing workflow. The detailed writing-pattern checklist has one owner in `review-writing`; other skills refer to that pass when useful and remain usable without it. A writing assessment may contribute to the existing independent review, but factual, behavioral and delivery checks remain with their owners. New formats should earn their own skill through a distinct output and evaluation method.

## Resources and verification

The packages carry conditional page-plan and copy-handoff templates with primary guidance. Check promises against source facts and actual destinations; keep public wording separate from evidence notes and private records. Preserve useful before/after copy, inspect the rendered result when requested, and obtain independent evaluation without calling an expert check a user study.

See the [product-page workflow](../workflows.md#plan-and-write-a-product-page) for composition with design and measurement, and [writing scenarios](../workflows.md#draft-and-review-writing) for articles, critiques and cross-domain prose review.
