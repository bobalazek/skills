# Brand workflows

Start at the missing result and reuse accepted strategy, visual direction and source assets. These routes describe possible handoffs, not a requirement to rebuild the whole brand.

## Define a position

“Help us choose what this offer should be known for, using these customer notes and actual capabilities.”

Use `define-brand-positioning` to inspect audience situations, alternatives, meaningful differences and proof. Open consequential choices wait for answers; explicitly delegated choices can proceed. Finish at the requested brief or options, with supported claims, proposal/acceptance status and a concrete investigation for missing evidence. Use `validate-product-idea` when the requested decision is whether demand merits further investment.

## Design or refine an identity

“We have accepted positioning and a logo we must keep. Prepare palette, typography and usage guidance for these touchpoints.”

Use `design-brand-identity` with the accepted brief, retained source assets and actual uses. Finish at the requested direction, guidelines or checked production assets. A guidelines-only request need not create a new logo, interface or design system.

## Continue into writing or interfaces

| Accepted input | Requested next result | Owner | Completion boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audience, promise and representative writing | Reusable language conventions | `define-writing-voice` | A supported voice profile; no invented founder opinions |
| Audience, promise and proof limits | Landing-page argument | `plan-landing-page` | Content sequence and evidence needs; visual layout remains separate |
| Visual identity and recurring consumer needs | Shared UI foundations | `build-design-system` | Checked token/component contracts and migration effects |
| Visual direction and settled screen structure | Interface composition | `design-interface` | Requested design/handoff or observed rendered surface |

Check that the next skill is available and its inputs are accepted; otherwise describe the plain action and missing prerequisite. Continue only work already authorized. Publishing assets, replacing live branding and buying resources require authority for those actions.

## Check the result

Trace strategic claims to the evidence and identity rules to the brief and intended uses. Retain the actual independent assessment where acceptance requires it. Keep proposed direction, inspected exports, owner approval and audience response distinct; a bounded fixture or expert opinion does not establish market performance.
